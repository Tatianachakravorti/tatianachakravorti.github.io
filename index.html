Hello, I’m Tatiana

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Alabama working with Dr. Qin Zhu, where my research focuses on human-centered and responsible AI, human-AI collaboration, and AI ethics and governance. I completed my PhD in Informatics at Penn State under the guidance of Dr. Sarah Rajtmajer in May 2026. Before coming to Penn State, I completed another PhD in India, which was in Electronics and Communication. I am particularly interested in understanding how AI systems are reshaping human judgment, decision-making, and professional practices, and how these systems can be designed and governed to support responsible and accountable AI use.

My research spans scientific and scholarly contexts, including research reproducibility and Open Science, AI ethics education, Human-AI collaboration, and the use of AI across research workflows. Using mixed-methods approaches, I study how people interact with AI systems, what influences their decisions to rely on or delegate tasks to AI, and how issues of transparency, accountability, trust, and professional judgment emerge in these interactions. Ultimately, I plan to work to inform the design and governance of AI systems that augment human capabilities while preserving meaningful human judgment and responsibility.

Research interests

Human-centered AI

I study how people use AI in research, how they decide when to rely on it, and what that means for their judgment.

AI Ethics

My work examines responsible AI use in peer review, research security, and AI ethics education.

Science of Science

I investigate reproducibility, replicability, Open Science, and how researchers assess scientific credibility.
